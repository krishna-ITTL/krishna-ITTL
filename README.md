### About

I build internal software for transformer manufacturing at Indo Tech Transformers: dispatch, testing, inspection tracking, hardware automation and HR tools. The goal is simple: replace manual, spreadsheet-driven processes with reliable applications the shop floor uses every day.

### Highlights

- **9 applications** in production use across manufacturing operations
- **Test planning** reduced from about 2 hours to 5 minutes
- **Hardware BOM preparation** reduced from about 30 minutes to 30 seconds
- **128 automated tests** covering the dispatch workflow
